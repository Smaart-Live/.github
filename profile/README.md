## What Is Smaart

Smaart is Rational Acoustics’ live-sound measurement platform. Touring engineers, install techs, and FOH mixers use Smaart live measurement because the PA is running and the clock is short. Smaart dual FFT is the engine: one channel is the reference (console output or generator), the other is a measurement mic in the house. Smaart transfer function is the plot that tells you whether the array you hung matches the feed. Smaart FOH analysis is the job on show day - delay, EQ, and a sanity RTA.

It is not Room EQ Wizard’s slow sweep-in-an-empty-cinema workflow, and it is not ARTA’s bench STEPS. Noise, music, or pink can drive the measurement while people are in the room. That is why system techs keep it on the same laptop as the console files they administer.

Smaart does not replace treatment, and it does not authorize tuning a venue you were not asked to touch. Measure systems you own, rent, or were hired to align. Keep traces with the show folder. A copied EQ from another tour is not a measurement.

## Starting with Smaart

Prerequisites: A licensed seat you administer, a two-channel (or more) interface, measurement mics, and a PA you are allowed to align.

1. **Prepare:** Place a mic at mix position, patch reference from the console you control, and set preamps so nothing clips.
2. **Configure:** Create a Smaart live measurement engine, assign reference and measurement inputs, and name the trace after the seat.
3. **Test:** Confirm Smaart dual FFT coherence is real, not a muted send or a swapped cable.
4. **Refine:** Use delay finder, then read Smaart transfer function before you cut EQ on the processors you already own.
5. **Apply:** Time-align and EQ the system you were hired to run, saving traces into the show folder.
6. **Maintain:** After the act, archive Smaart FOH analysis with the console file, and do not leave mics in the aisle.
